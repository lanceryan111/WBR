Subject: PAT Deployment Process and Support Expectations

Hi Team,

Following today’s discussion regarding the Thematic PAT deployment, I’d like to clarify a few expectations around the deployment process and our support going forward.

We strongly recommend using the established automated deployment process for application deployments. This is aligned with our internal standards and industry best practices, and helps ensure deployments are consistent, repeatable, and properly supported.

We understand that the Thematic project is working against a particularly urgent timeline, as has been highlighted on several occasions. Given the circumstances, we understand the decision to proceed with a manual deployment for this initial PAT deployment. However, this should be treated as an exception rather than the standard approach.

It is also important to note that the Modernization team did not receive any advance notice or heads-up regarding this PAT deployment. We only became aware of it when questions and support requests started coming in during the deployment. Going forward, please involve us ahead of time if our support is expected, particularly when the deployment deviates from the documented or standard process. This will allow us to review the approach and identify any prerequisites or potential issues beforehand.

As Marcin highlighted in the chat, deployments or implementations that do not follow the established standards should not be expected to receive ongoing support from the Modernization team afterward. We are happy to help the team get onto the supported path, but we also need to ensure that the agreed processes and standards are being followed.

We have already shared documentation covering the required setup and deployment steps, including examples for common scenarios. Please review the documentation carefully before reaching out for assistance, as many of the questions being raised are already addressed there. This will help reduce unnecessary back-and-forth and allow us to focus our support on issues that genuinely require troubleshooting or additional clarification.

We’re happy to help with any questions that remain after reviewing the documentation and to support the team in transitioning to the automated deployment process for future deployments.

Thanks,
Fei
